Why Fruteinz

Built around what snackers actually told us.

Protein matters.

In our survey, protein content emerged as the single strongest attribute snackers look for in a protein snack.

Clean label matters.

About 86% of respondents rated “no artificial additives, preservatives or added sugar” a 4 or 5 out of 5 in importance.

Taste can’t be an afterthought.

Our in-depth interviews flagged bitterness and taste as a recurring frustration with existing protein snacks.

Convenience matters.

Snackers told us they want protein that fits into busy moments — exactly what a bite-sized, grab-and-go format is for.

Based on primary consumer research conducted for our Fundamentals of Marketing project — a 43-respondent survey (41 on brand recall) and 5 in-depth interviews.
